How Till works
  1. Set your rulesChoose what the agent may buy, spending caps, and when Till should ask you.
  2. Connect a shopping agentThe agent sends proposals to POST /api/orders. It cannot call PayPal or mark an order approved.
  3. Review the outcomeTill allows, blocks, or asks you to review. PayPal still asks the buyer to authorize each checkout.

A PayPal order is marked complete only after Till verifies the capture event and checks its amount against the decision.
